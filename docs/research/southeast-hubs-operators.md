# Anchorage to Juneau, Ketchikan and Sitka, and on to Southeast communities: operators

Question: which real operators move a technician and a loadout from Anchorage to Juneau (JNU), Ketchikan (KTN) or Sitka (SIT), on to communities around each hub, and by sea, and what does each publish about itself?

Read on 24 Nov 2026

## How to read this

- **Confirmed** means the fact was read on the operator's own website (URL after the fact), or on the State's own dot.alaska.gov page for the ferry.
- **Lead** means the operator was named on a third-party page (news, Wikipedia, search results) and is not confirmed.
- "Not published" means the operator's page, as read, did not say. Nothing was filled from memory.
- Pages were read through an automated fetcher that summarises each page, so details marked "re-read" are worth a human check against the live page before they go in a record. No browser was used.
- Waybill = the app's word for the tracking number; each operator's own word is noted.
- Where the fetcher gave two answers for the same fact (different pages, or a retry), both are shown and marked "conflict".
- No person, client, telecom company or site is named in this note. (Samson's contact page names staff; they are left out.)
- Anchorage addresses for Alaska Air Cargo, NAC and Lynden Air Cargo are already confirmed in `docs/research/anchorage-bethel-operators.md` and are not repeated.

## Alaska Marine Highway System (AMHS), State ferry

- **Name:** Alaska Marine Highway System, Alaska DOT&PF. Website: https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/ (state service; its dot.alaska.gov pages count as primary).
- **Mode / carries:** sea; people and vehicles. Freight: "Unaccompanied baggage is not allowed on board AMHS vessels; it is considered freight" and must go through a commercial carrier. Commercial vehicles are booked through the Commercial Services Desk; cargo vans and trailers up to 48 feet accepted, over 48 feet cannot be accommodated. https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/policies.shtml
- **Routes touching the hubs:** mainline Bellingham to Ketchikan to Wrangell to Petersburg to Juneau, with a branch to Haines, Skagway and Sitka. Juneau scheduled routes include Angoon, Gustavus, Hoonah, Haines, Kake, Pelican, Tenakee and Skagway; Ketchikan shuttle to Metlakatla. Sample running times: Bellingham to Ketchikan 38 hours; Ketchikan to Juneau 20 hours (listed under Gulf of Alaska, may not apply in winter); Juneau to Sitka 9 h 30 min; Juneau to Hoonah 3 h 30 min; Ketchikan to Metlakatla 45 min. https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/route.shtml . Anchorage: no route read.
- **Seasons:** Winter 2026/27 proposed schedule runs 1 Oct 2026 to 30 Apr 2027 and drops Yakutat and "cross gulf" routes (route-guide page text, as read; the schedule was described as proposed). https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/route.shtml
- **Phone and hours:**
  - Reservations and customer service: `"(800) 642-0066 Toll Free"` and `"(907) 465-3941 Phone | (907) 465-8824 Fax"`; "Monday-Friday, 8:00am - 3:30pm AST". Policies page gave 8:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. and also 7:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. (**conflict**; re-read). https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/contact.shtml , https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/policies.shtml
  - Commercial services and freight: no phone listed; email dot.amhs.commercial@alaska.gov, labelled "All commercial equipment". https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/contact.shtml
- **Dangerous goods:** "Explosives, highly flammable materials, corrosives, etc. may not be transported on any AMHS vessel." Bottled gas such as RV propane allowed if turned off and sealed by an AMHS employee. Vehicles leaking fuel refused. Lithium batteries as cargo: Not addressed (page covers EVs/PHEVs only; damaged or recalled ones denied boarding). https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/policies.shtml
- **Weight/size:** standard vehicles up to 8 feet wide; wider ones charged about 125% (103-107 in) or 150% (108 in and over). A vehicle weight-restriction PDF is linked but was not read. https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/policies.shtml
- **Waybill:** Not published (no freight booking form read).
- **Online reservations** close 4 hours before sailing. https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/policies.shtml
