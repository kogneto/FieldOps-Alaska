# Anchorage to Bethel to Y-K Delta villages: operators

Question: which real operators move a technician and a loadout from Anchorage to Bethel, on to villages around Bethel, and back, and what does each publish about itself?

Read on 24 Nov 2026

## How to read this

- **Confirmed** means the fact was read on the operator's own website (URL after the fact).
- **Lead** means the operator was named on a third-party page (news, Wikipedia, search results) and is not confirmed.
- "Not published" means the operator's page, as read, did not say. Nothing was filled from memory.
- Pages were read through an automated fetcher that summarises each page, so quotes are short and a few details (marked "re-read") are worth a human check against the live page before they go in a record.
- Waybill = the app's word for the tracking number; each operator's own word is noted.
- No client, telecom company or site is named in this note.

Y-K Delta villages: Not published (its Bethel news page says bypass-mail goods are "flown on to nearby villages" but names no operator or village), https://public.alaskacargo.com/cargo-news/people-of-cargo-bethel
- **Phone:** Call center, toll free 1-800-225-2752 (labelled "Toll Free (Call Center)" at (800) 225-2752). Daily 5:00 am to 9:00 pm PST. https://public.alaskacargo.com/contact-us . The same 1-800-225-2752 is listed on both station pages.
- **Email:** Not published as an address; the contact page links an "Email Us" form. https://public.alaskacargo.com/contact-us
- **Hours (Anchorage station):** GoldStreak and Pet Connect pick-up and drop-off 24 hours, 7 days. Air freight pick-up/drop-off Mon-Fri 5:00 am to 10:00 pm, Sat 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, Sun closed. https://public.alaskacargo.com/network-locations/anchorage
- **Hours (Bethel station), pick-up and drop-off:** Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat 2:00 pm to 5:00 pm; Tue 10:00 am to 5:00 pm; Sun closed. https://public.alaskacargo.com/network-locations/bethel
- **Seasonal limits:** Not published as a schedule. The Bethel news page says weather sometimes delays incoming shipments. https://public.alaskacargo.com/cargo-news/people-of-cargo-bethel . Bethel station: storage fees assessed after five days, excluding weekends and holidays. https://public.alaskacargo.com/network-locations/bethel
- **GoldStreak:** "Fastest way to ship time-critical packages under 150 lbs." Next available flight; pick-up available within one hour of arrival. Maximum per piece 90 linear inches (L+W+H). https://public.alaskacargo.com/services . Listed as a service at both Anchorage and Bethel stations (URLs above).
- **General limits:** Maximum 300 lb per piece in the lower cargo compartments; "Certain regional destinations have a limit of 150 pounds per piece" (which ones: not stated). https://public.alaskacargo.com/faqs . Pallet/tub acceptance: Not published on pages read.
- **Dangerous goods:** Anchorage: accepted, fully regulated, shipper declaration required. https://public.alaskacargo.com/network-locations/anchorage . Bethel lists "Dangerous Goods" as a service but gives no terms. https://public.alaskacargo.com/network-locations/bethel . Lithium: installed in or packed with equipment (UN3481, UN3091) accepted; standalone (UN3480, UN3090) accepted only on freighter aircraft; damaged or recycling batteries not accepted. https://public.alaskacargo.com/what-we-ship/dangerous-goods . Compressed gas and fuel: no specific acceptance rule on that page.

- **Email:** customerserviceagents@nac.aero (freight inquiries); insidesales@nac.aero (quotes and charters). https://nac.aero/contact/
- **Hours (Anchorage cargo facility):** Mon-Fri 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.; Sat 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.; Sun closed. https://nac.aero/contact/ . Bethel hours: Not published.
- **Seasonal limits:** Not published. Page says "Actual flight times may vary due to daily operational constraints such as weather." https://www.nac.aero/?p=2401
- **Schedule:** service schedule effective 05/16/2026 lists Anchorage-Bethel flights 730/731 (M, T, Th), 732/733 (W, F), 734/735 (Su). The fetch summary of the table was hard to read, so re-read times on https://www.nac.aero/?p=2401 before use.
- **Services:** general air cargo and priority services; oversized freight, vehicles, declared hazardous materials. https://nac.aero/ . Customer service agents can arrange local pick-up and delivery in Anchorage and Bethel (from third-party search snippet of NAC's own site; not re-read on the page).
- **Weight/size per piece:** Not published on pages read. Pallet/tub: Not published.
- **Dangerous goods:** accepted under IATA rules since May 1, 2023, with advance notice; lithium batteries "only under the provisions of IATA"; propane must be declared; fuel-related hazmat must be declared; camp stoves, tanks and motors must be empty of fuel; shipper's declaration required. https://nac.aero/dangerous-goods/ . - **Where the technician meets it:**
  - Anchorage: Cargo Facility, 3900 Old International Airport Road, Anchorage, AK 99502. https://nac.aero/contact/
