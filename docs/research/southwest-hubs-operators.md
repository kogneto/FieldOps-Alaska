## How to read this

- **Confirmed** means the fact was read on the operator's own website (URL after the fact), or on the State's own dot.alaska.gov page for the ferry.
- **Lead** means the operator was named only on a third-party page (news, Wikipedia, airport or travel directory, search snippet) and is not confirmed.
- "Not published" means the page, as read, did not say. Nothing was filled from memory.
- Pages were read through an automated fetcher that summarises each page, except the PDFs marked "read as PDF", which were opened directly. A few details are marked "re-read": worth a human check against the live page before they go in a record.
- Phone numbers: where the page shows one number with one caption, it is given as read. Where several numbers sat together, the raw text order is quoted. Where the fetcher's pairing could be off, it says "pairing unclear".
- Waybill = the app's word; each operator's own word is noted.
- Grant Aviation is skipped (the maintainer has read it by hand). Ravn Alaska could not be read (403), see Leads.
- No individual, client, telecom company or site is named.

## Alaska Marine Highway System (state ferry)

- **Name:** Alaska Marine Highway System (AMHS), Alaska DOT&PF. Website: https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/ . Mode: ferry; people and vehicles. Primary source (state page).
- **Reservations:** 1-800-642-0066, reservations center "from 8:00 AM to 3:30 PM, Monday through Friday" (Alaska DOT&PF page, re-read). The Kodiak Island community page lists (907) 228-6886 as the AMHS Reservations Call Center. https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/ , https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/comm/old_ouz_ori.shtml
- **Kodiak (ADQ) terminal:** 100 Marine Way, Kodiak, AK 99615; "Terminal Contact: (907) 486-3800"; hours: Not published. Kodiak is about 136 nautical miles southwest of Homer, "nine-and-a-half hour ferry ride". The Tustumena docks at Pier 1 behind the terminal; the Kennicott at Pier 2, about a mile away. https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/comm/kodiak.shtml
- **Kodiak Island ports:** Old Harbor (terminal at the end of Walden Point Road, 15 miles from town), Ouzinkie, Port Lions. Sailing frequency and season: not on that page.
- **Vehicles and baggage:** "can transport any form of conveyance with wheels that has roll-on/roll-off capabilities"; walk-on passengers up to 100 pounds of hand-carried baggage. Freight, pallets and tubs as freight: Not published on pages read. https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/
- **Season / schedule:** the draft Winter 2026-2027 plan (Oct 1 2026 to Apr 30 2027, read as PDF) lists M/V Tustumena on the "Southwest Route" Oct 1 to Jan 15, then overhaul Jan 15 through Apr 30, with the M/V Tustumena back "May 01, SW" on the operating-plan chart. The plan is labelled DRAFT (revision 27.3, 04/17/26) and the State says it was proposed for public comment. https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/doc/winter_considerations_2026-2027.pdf . Service notices on https://dot.alaska.gov/amhs/ conflict about the Tustumena's return (one says it resumes its published schedule on Thursday, November 26, 2026; another says all sailings are cancelled through November 28 and resume from Kodiak on November 28), so confirm with AMHS before travel. Kodiak-Homer sailings per week: Not published on pages read.
- **King Salmon and Dillingham:** not served by AMHS on pages read.
