# Anchorage to Nome, Kotzebue and Utqiaġvik, and on to villages: operators

Question: which real operators move a technician and a loadout from Anchorage to Nome (OME), Kotzebue (OTZ) and Utqiaġvik / Barrow (BRW), on to the villages around each hub, and back, and what does each publish about itself?

Read on 24 Nov 2026

## How to read this

- **Confirmed** means the fact was read on the operator's own website (URL after the fact).
- **Lead** means the operator was named on a third-party page, or only in a search snippet of its own site that was not opened, and is not confirmed.
- "Not published" means the page, as read, did not say. Nothing was filled from memory.
- Pages were read through an automated fetcher that summarises each page. Phone numbers are quoted as `caption / number` in the order the fetcher reported. Where the fetcher was not asked for raw order, or the pairing could not be seen, the entry says "pairing not verified" or "pairing unclear". Re-read those in a browser before they go in a record.
- Anchorage addresses for Alaska Air Cargo, NAC, Lynden Air Cargo and Ryan Air are already confirmed in `docs/research/anchorage-bethel-operators.md` and are not repeated.
- Waybill = the app's word for the tracking number; each operator's own word is noted.
- Names of station managers and other individuals on the pages were left out, and so were two individual-looking email addresses (a Lynden Nome contact and a Lynden Air Cargo-AML sales contact). No client, telecom company or site is named.
- Region note: this note uses "hub" for Nome, Kotzebue and Utqiaġvik. The sources spell the third as Barrow (Alaska Air Cargo, NAC, Alaska Airlines) or Utqiagvik (Wright, Lynden).
