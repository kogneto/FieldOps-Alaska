# FieldOps Alaska

A field companion for telecom technicians who travel to remote sites in Alaska. This glossary fixes the words the app and its docs use for the people, places and paperwork involved.

## Language

### Places

**Site**:
A place with telecom equipment that technicians travel to and work at, such as a repeater, a fiber hut, a cell tower or an earth station. A technician types in their own sites, and they are kept only on their phone. No site is ever a record.
_Avoid_: Location, facility, jobsite

**Region**:
A named part of Alaska used to group operators. An operator serves one or more.
_Avoid_: Area, district

### People and organizations

**Technician**:
The person who uses FieldOps for their own trips. Anyone can be one: there is no sign-in and no group to belong to.
_Avoid_: User, crew member, tech

**Operator**:
A business that moves technicians or freight by air, sea or land.
_Avoid_: Carrier, transport provider, charter (as a name for the business)

**Client**:
The organization the work is done for. It sets a site's confidentiality and holds its access secrets.
_Avoid_: Operator, network operator, carrier, customer

**Maintainer**:
The one named person who keeps the records current and decides each record's confidence.
_Avoid_: Admin, dispatcher, editor

### Travel

**Trip**:
One outing by a technician from home base to one or more sites and back, with its own dates, loadout and checklists. The technician builds and ships the loadout, travels out, makes sure it arrived, does the work, packs up and ships it home, travels home, and makes sure it all came back.
_Avoid_: Visit, job, run

**Checklist**:
A list a technician ticks through at one point in a trip. Each trip carries its own. The maintainer writes the ones for arranging a trip beforehand; four more run over the trip's loadout: the pre-loadout, the arrival checklist, the departure pre-loadout and the return checklist.
_Avoid_: Prep list, kit list

**Route**:
One way of reaching a site: with an operator, or driving yourself. The technician chooses one for each site on a trip.
_Avoid_: Way in, access method, reach-by

**Home base**:
Where a technician sets out from and comes back to, whether for more parts or for R&R.
_Avoid_: Shop, depot, headquarters

**Loadout**:
The tools and parts a trip needs at its sites, built on a pallet or in a tub. It usually ships with an operator, apart from the technician, both out and back.
_Avoid_: Kit, gear list

**Waybill**:
The number an operator gives a shipment of the loadout. The technician types it in at the check the loadout ships at.
_Avoid_: Tracking number, airbill, AWB

**Pre-loadout**:
The checklist run at home base while the loadout is built, before it ships.
_Avoid_: Packing list, load plan

**Arrival checklist**:
The checklist run at a site to make sure the whole loadout arrived. A trip has one for each site.
_Avoid_: Receiving check, inventory

**Departure pre-loadout**:
The checklist run at a site while the loadout is packed up to ship, on to the next site or home. A trip has one for each site.
_Avoid_: Pack-out, demob

**Return checklist**:
The checklist run back at home base to make sure everything made it back. A trip has one, and it comes last.
_Avoid_: Check-in, reconciliation

**Lodging**:
The hotel or cabin a technician stays in during a trip.
_Avoid_: Accommodation, hotel (as the general term)

**Travel document**:
An image a technician keeps with a trip, such as a boarding pass. It is theirs, not a record, and is kept only on their phone.
_Avoid_: Attachment, file, paperwork

### Getting into a site

**Qualification**:
Something a technician must hold to work at a site: a certification, a training record, a medical clearance or an issued badge.
_Avoid_: Credential, cert (as the general term)

**Qualification record**:
A technician's own list of the qualifications they hold and the month each expires, kept only on their phone.
_Avoid_: Profile, credentials, cert list

**Access secret**:
A gate code, key or lock combination that lets someone into a site. It is held by the client, and FieldOps has no place to write one down: not in a record, and not in a typed-in site. The one exception is a travel document, which is the technician's own image and may show one.
_Avoid_: Credential, gate code (as the general term)

**Confidentiality**:
The client's instruction for how a site may be talked about, which the technician sets when typing the site in. An **Open** site may be named freely. A **Confidential** site stays with the technicians working it, except that an operator may be told the place and date needed to get there, never what the site is or who the client is.
_Avoid_: Public, restricted, client-confidential, classification

### Trusting a record

**Record**:
The maintainer's entry for one operator, drawn from what the operator publishes about itself. Only operators have records. A technician's own sites, trips and travel documents are not records.
_Avoid_: Listing, entry, profile

**Confidence**:
The maintainer's judgment of how far a record can be relied on. High means act on it; medium means confirm by phone before committing travel or money; low means treat it as a lead only.
_Avoid_: Reliability, trust level

**Verified date**:
The date the maintainer last confirmed a whole record against its source.
_Avoid_: Last updated, last checked

**Stale**:
Said of a record whose verified date is older than the freshness cutoff, currently 60 days.
_Avoid_: Outdated, expired

### In the app

**Saved list**:
The operators a technician has starred for quick access. It has no bearing on what is available offline.
_Avoid_: Offline pack, offline list, favorites

**Feedback note**:
A short anonymous message a technician sends from the app, optionally tied to one operator. It never carries a site.
_Avoid_: Report, ticket, change request
